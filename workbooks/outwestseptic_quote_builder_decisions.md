# Decisions before quote automation

The manual worksheet is available now. The following decisions are unresolved; no values have been invented to make an automated workbook appear complete.

| Decision | Responsible reviewer | Evidence needed | Behavior until resolved |
|---|---|---|---|
| Offered scope and approved line-item catalog | Business owner | Current service scope and item definitions | Enter project-specific descriptions manually |
| Price inputs and rounding policy | Business owner / estimator | Dated supplier and labor bases; currency policy | Blank prices; label allowances and reconcile manually |
| Tax classification, taxable base and rates | Appropriate tax reviewer | Actual contract/property facts and applicable Comptroller guidance | No automatic tax calculation or final release with unresolved tax |
| Deposit, payment methods, validity, warranty and change terms | Business owner and appropriate adviser | Approved terms with version/date | Blank terms; no standard percentage or warranty promise |
| Quote numbering, revisions and approval | Business owner | Unique-ID and approval process | Assign manually and retain revision history |
| Public branding, verified contacts and authorized signatory | Business owner | Approved current details | Project name only; no assumed license or signature |
| Customer-data storage and delivery | Business owner | Authorized recipients, retention and access policy | Keep completed forms private; no email or export automation |

A future implementation must demonstrate totals, exclusions, duplicate-fee prevention, unresolved-input handling and stable revisions using clearly synthetic examples. Review exported output before claiming an XLSM or PDF workflow exists.
