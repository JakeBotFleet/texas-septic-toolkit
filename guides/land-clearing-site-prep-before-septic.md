# What should be settled before site work near a proposed OSSF?

> Coordinate the evaluated disposal area, approved layout, and construction access before clearing, grading, or placing fill.

Reviewed **2026-10-18**.

Use this planning checklist with the evaluator, designer, installer, and relevant authority:

1. Identify the proposed system and disposal area on the site plan.
2. Confirm how access, storage, grading, drainage, and other planned improvements interact with that area.
3. Record existing utilities, wells, easements, water features, and other constraints for review.
4. Agree on which work requires approval and how changes will be documented.
5. Keep the construction team working from the same approved plan.

TCEQ's [owner advice](https://www.tceq.texas.gov/permitting/ossf/ossfadvice.html) recommends documenting selections and locations and avoiding design changes made during construction. Its [permit guidance](https://www.tceq.texas.gov/permitting/ossf/ossfpermits.html) explains the authority's role.

Near Possum Kingdom Lake, also review the [BRA jurisdiction guide](possum-kingdom-lake-septic-considerations.md); related lake/land work may involve separate approvals. This page establishes no contractor equipment capability, clearing price, or guaranteed preparation method.
