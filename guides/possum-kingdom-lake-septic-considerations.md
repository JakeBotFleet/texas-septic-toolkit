# Who should confirm septic jurisdiction near Possum Kingdom Lake?

> Ask the Brazos River Authority and the relevant permitting office to confirm the parcel's jurisdiction before selecting forms, fees, or a system.

Sources reviewed **2026-10-18**. This guide intentionally does not draw a jurisdiction boundary or declare a parcel suitable.

## What the BRA source establishes

Section 17.A of [BRA's lake regulations](https://brazos.org/about-us/reservoirs/possum-kingdom-lake/lake-regulations) identifies BRA as a TCEQ authorized agent in its jurisdiction and directs people installing, repairing, replacing, or modifying OSSFs to coordinate with the lake office about permit requirements.

The same regulations, section 4.C, define Possum Kingdom Lake's boundary using the **1,000-foot mean sea level contour (BRA datum)**. That is an elevation reference. It is not a 1,000-foot horizontal setback, and it does not establish a universal OSSF buffer around a user's map point.

The source reviewed does not establish a complete parcel-level OSSF boundary using a “2,000 feet or 1,000-foot contour, whichever is more inclusive” expression. Obtain an authority determination for the property.

## Prepare for a jurisdiction confirmation

Send the office a parcel/legal description, survey or location map, existing permit information if available, and a description of the proposed work. Ask:

- Does BRA or another entity permit the proposed OSSF work?
- Which current local OSSF order, application, and fee schedule apply?
- Which evaluation and planning material are required?
- What inspections, approvals, and maintenance conditions would apply to the proposed configuration?
- Are separate approvals needed for related work on BRA land or at the lake?

Keep the authority's response with the project record. A dock permit, lake contour, postal city, or county name should not be substituted for that response.

## Jurisdiction does not select treatment

Being near the lake does not by itself establish that an aerobic treatment unit is required, nor that conventional disposal is acceptable. TCEQ's [system-selection guidance](https://www.tceq.texas.gov/permitting/ossf/ossfsystems.html) evaluates treatment and disposal together with site conditions. The local authority must also confirm its requirements.

Do not budget a fixed “lake surcharge,” assume a contractor can perform part of the work, or treat a calculator output as an approved plan. Use written scopes and the [tool input/source checklist](tool-inputs-and-sources.md).

For a Palo Pinto County project outside BRA's confirmed jurisdiction, see the [county preparation guide](tceq-ossf-permitting-palo-pinto-county.md).
