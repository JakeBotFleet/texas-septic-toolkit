# What should I confirm before OSSF installation?

> Confirm the authorization, approved planning material, inspection sequence, and responsibilities before work begins.

Reviewed **2026-10-18**.

Use a preconstruction discussion to record:

- The approved system and plan revision being installed.
- The person responsible for each task and required qualification.
- Access, utility coordination, work-area boundaries, and property protection.
- Who schedules inspections and which components must remain available for inspection.
- How proposed changes and unexpected conditions receive approval.
- What completion, operating, maintenance, and as-built records will be delivered.

Use the authority's directions for inspection and covering work. A repository checklist cannot set those requirements or promise a one-day installation. Start with [TCEQ permitting guidance](https://www.tceq.texas.gov/permitting/ossf/ossfpermits.html) and the [Palo Pinto preparation guide](tceq-ossf-permitting-palo-pinto-county.md) where applicable.
