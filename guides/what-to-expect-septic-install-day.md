# What to expect when getting a septic system installed in North Texas

Out West Septic, outwestseptic.com, TCEQ OSSF Installer I, License OS0041298, Lone Camp, TX, 940.452.0598

If you've never had a septic system installed, the day-of can feel like a lot of heavy equipment showing up at your house. This is what actually happens on install day for a conventional gravity system in Palo Pinto County or rural North Texas. Start to finish, from the time Out West Septic pulls in to the time we leave.

> Quick answer: A conventional septic install takes 1 to 3 days depending on soil, rock, and drainfield length. The excavator digs the tank hole and the lateral trenches, the tank is set, the laterals are laid, the DR inspects before backfill, and Out West Septic backfills and rough-restores the site. The yard is disturbed for a few weeks until the seed takes.

## Before install day

By the time we arrive, the following should already be done:

- Permit approved by the county DR (Palo Pinto County Public Works, or the equivalent in Hood, Erath, Stephens, Young, Jack, or Eastland County).
- Site plan finalized and staked.
- Access path clear from driveway to tank and drainfield areas.
- Building pad prepped (if part of the same scope).
- Customer or representative available by phone.

## Day 1: tank hole and lateral trenches

We arrive with:

- A 20-ton excavator
- Dump truck or trailer for hauling the tank and for hauling out spoils
- Hand tools, transit, tape measure
- Pipe, gravel, fabric, distribution box

The morning sequence:

1. Walk the site to confirm tank and drainfield locations.
2. Excavate the tank hole to the depth specified on the approved site plan. Tank holes are typically 7-9 feet deep depending on the tank size and inlet depth.
3. Set the tank in the hole using the excavator. Level the tank. Check the inlet and outlet elevations.
4. Excavate the lateral trenches to the depth and length per the design. Each trench is typically 24-36 inches wide and 18-36 inches deep.
5. Lay the pipe in the trenches, set the distribution box, place the inspection ports.
6. Backfill the lateral trenches with the specified gravel and fabric per the design.

## Day 2: lateral pipe, DR inspection, prep

1. Lay the gravel in the trenches.
2. Install the geotextile fabric.
3. Connect distribution box to the laterals.
4. Call DR for inspection.
5. DR walks the install (1-3 business day lead time).

## Day 3: backfill and restoration

1. Backfill the tank hole with the spoil that was set aside. Compact in lifts.
2. Backfill the lateral trenches with the remaining spoil.
3. Rough grade the disturbed area so water drains away from the tank and the home.
4. Topsoil over the disturbed area. We stockpile this from the original excavation.
5. Seed and straw the disturbed area. Out West Septic uses a native grass seed blend appropriate for the site.

The yard will look rough for a few weeks. The seed takes 2-4 weeks depending on weather. Weeds will show up first. The grass fills in.

## If we hit rock

If we hit rock during excavation, Out West Septic stops and calls you before continuing. The rock adder is itemized on your quote. We don't surprise you with extra cost. Common outcomes:

- Shallow rock in the tank hole. Usually means a deeper excavation or a relocation. Out West Septic evaluates and proposes.
- Rock in the lateral trenches. Usually means the rock adder applies per cubic yard. The trenches get reframed to stay above the rock where possible.
- Significant rock across the site. Usually means a redesign conversation. Sometimes a mound system is the right answer. Sometimes the tank location shifts.

The site visit before the quote tells us roughly what to expect. The actual encounter tells us what the install really costs.

## How long does it take

For a typical residential conventional install in Palo Pinto County:

- Day 1: tank hole, tank set, lateral excavation.
- Day 2: lateral pipe and gravel, distribution box, inspection ports, DR inspection if scheduled.
- Day 3: backfill, rough grade, topsoil, seed, straw.

If we hit rock or the drainfield is unusually long, add a day or two. If everything goes smoothly, some installs finish in two days.

The homeowner should expect heavy equipment on site for 2-4 days. We work around your driveway and access as much as possible. If you have pets or kids, keep them away from the equipment area.

## After install: the first few weeks

After we leave:

- Water use is normal. You can use the plumbing in the house as soon as the tank is set and the lines are connected.
- DO NOT drive over tank or drainfield. Compacted soil reduces drainage and can damage the system. Out West Septic marks tank and drainfield locations on every install.
- DO NOT plant trees or shrubs over the drainfield. Roots will find the moist soil.
- DO watch the seed. Water if needed to keep the seedbed moist. The first mow happens when the grass is 3-4 inches tall.
- DO call with questions. Tanner is available at 940.452.0598.

## The first pump

After install, plan to pump the tank:

- 3 years if you have a garbage disposal.
- 4 years if no disposal but you have a water softener.
- 5 years if neither.
