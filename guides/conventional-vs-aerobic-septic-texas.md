# How should I compare conventional and aerobic septic options?

> Start with the evaluated site and approved treatment/disposal combination. A county name, lot acreage, or perc threshold cannot select a system.

Reviewed **2026-10-18**.

A septic tank provides primary treatment; an aerobic treatment unit is one possible secondary-treatment option. Treatment and effluent disposal are separate parts of the decision. TCEQ lists multiple disposal methods with different site limits and professional requirements. [TCEQ system-selection guidance](https://www.tceq.texas.gov/permitting/ossf/ossfsystems.html)

Use these questions with the evaluator and designer:

- Which treatment and disposal combinations fit the evaluated conditions?
- Which restrictions or footnotes apply to that combination?
- What usable area remains after the applicable separations?
- What operation, maintenance, power, and access needs follow from the proposed design?
- What does the local authority require before construction and operation?

Compare written installation scopes and ongoing obligations, not an unsupported statewide price range. See [quote comparison](septic-costs-north-texas-2026.md), [soil research](palo-pinto-county-soil-types-septic.md), and [tool limitations](tool-inputs-and-sources.md).
