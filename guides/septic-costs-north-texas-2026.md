## What drives the cost on a new install

The five factors that move the price the most, in roughly this order:

1. Tank size. Bigger tank = more cost. Three bedrooms needs 1,000 gal. Four needs 1,250. Six needs 1,500. Seven-plus adds 250 gal per additional bedroom.
2. Drainfield length. This is the big swing factor. Permeable sandy soil needs short laterals. Tight clay or rocky ground needs much longer laterals to absorb the same daily flow.
3. Soil. If we're cutting through rock or caliche, the trench excavation slows down and we burn through wear on the equipment. That cost is real and it shows up on the quote.
4. Site access. Tight driveways, long hauls, steep slopes, or trees that need to come out before we can get equipment in all add cost.
5. Permit fees. County pass-through. Usually $200-$400 in the counties we work.

## What adds cost on a real job

In our experience, the top reasons a quote climbs from the base range to the high end:

- Hitting rock. Limestone shelves are common in Palo Pinto County. The rock adder is itemized on every quote. Typical adder is $50-$150 per cubic yard of rock we have to remove.
- Slope work. Drainfield trenching on a slope requires careful layout and sometimes terracing. Modest slope adds 15-30%.
- Tight access. If we can't get the excavator close to the install area, we have to hand-dig or use smaller equipment. Adds 10-25%.
- Long haul-off. If the spoils have to go far, that's loader time. Adds modest cost.
- Permit complexity. Lots inside Possum Kingdom Lake BRA jurisdiction need extra steps. See our [PK Lake septic guide](./possum-kingdom-lake-septic-considerations.md).

## What an inspection actually costs

A real estate septic inspection in North Texas runs $250-$500. Out West Septic's inspection includes:

- Visual inspection of the tank (water level, baffles, risers if present).
- Visual inspection of the drainfield (surfacing, odors, distribution box).
- Check for code compliance on setbacks and permits.
- A written report Out West Septic signs as TCEQ OSSF Installer I (OS0041298).
- Pass / Conditional Pass / Fail verdict with recommended next steps.

For a real estate transaction, the inspection report is usually due within 5-10 days of the contract date. We turn reports around in 48 hours.

## What a repair costs when something has failed

The most common repair calls we get in Palo Pinto County:

- Backups into the house. Usually a clogged line between the house and the tank, or a flooded drainfield. Cleaning the line runs $250-$600. Drainfield remediation runs $3,000-$8,000 depending on how bad the field is.
- Slow drains throughout the house. Usually the tank is overdue for pumping or the drainfield is saturated. Pump first ($200-$400 referred to a local pumper), then re-evaluate.
- Wet spots over the drainfield. Almost always drainfield failure. Sometimes a localized repair (one lateral) saves the system. Sometimes full replacement is the honest answer.
- Sewage odor outdoors. Could be a tank baffle issue, a cracked lid, or a vent problem. Baffle replacement runs $300-$800. Lid or crack work runs $200-$600.
- Tank riser replacement. If the tank was installed without risers and you need access for pumping or inspection, riser install runs $500-$1,200 depending on tank depth.

## Hidden costs to budget for

Beyond the install or repair number, budget for:

- Initial pump truck visit (referred to a local pumper. Out West Septic does not run a pump truck). Budget $200-$400.
- First-year maintenance. See our [homeowner maintenance guide](./septic-maintenance-schedule-homeowners.md).
- Slope restoration. Topsoil and seed over the disturbed area is usually included but confirm on your quote.
- Power for the system. Conventional systems have no electrical draw. Aerobic systems (which Out West Septic does not install) draw continuously.
