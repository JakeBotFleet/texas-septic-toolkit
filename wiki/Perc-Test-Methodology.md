# Can a perc log select an OSSF or size a drainfield?

> No. A log can preserve observations, but it cannot replace the required site evaluation or establish a design loading rate.

Record observations for their stated purpose. A field log does not by itself document an approved evaluation or a permitting procedure.

TCEQ's [system-selection guidance](https://www.tceq.texas.gov/permitting/ossf/ossfsystems.html) starts with evaluation by a licensed site evaluator or professional engineer and considers soil classes, groundwater, restrictive horizons, and other conditions. A claimed “Installer III” credential is not part of the categories on TCEQ's [OSSF licensing page](https://www.tceq.texas.gov/licensing/licenses/ossflic).

If retaining a field log, record who collected it, location, date, method, raw readings, units, conditions, and purpose. Do not label observations as an approved evaluation. The evaluator and authority determine what evidence is needed.
