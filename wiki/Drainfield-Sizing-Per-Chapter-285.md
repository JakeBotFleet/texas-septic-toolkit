# What does an absorptive-area calculation establish?

> It checks arithmetic for supplied daily flow and soil application rate. It does not establish site suitability, trench geometry, or an approved layout.

See [tool inputs and formula provenance](../guides/tool-inputs-and-sources.md) for the source edition, units, example, and current-rule verification gap.

The [drainfield script](../scripts/outwestseptic_drainfield_calc.py) must be used with documented inputs. Retain the source and professional selection of those inputs. A timed reading in minutes per inch is not a loading rate in gallons per square foot per day.

For evidence needed before choosing inputs, see [soil research and evaluation](../guides/palo-pinto-county-soil-types-septic.md). Keep any product-specific sizing method, sidewall treatment, restrictions, and layout decisions with reviewed planning material.
