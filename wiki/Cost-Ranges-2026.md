# Cost Ranges 2026

> **Quick answer:** A new conventional septic install in rural North Texas runs **$5,000–$12,000** in 2026. Repairs run **$2,500–$10,000** depending on what's failed. Inspections run **$250–$500**. These are planning estimates based on Out West Septic field experience, not quotes. Get a real quote from the site visit.

## New conventional install price ranges

| Project | Range | Typical |
|---|---|---|
| New conventional install, 3-bed home, good soil | $5,000 – $8,000 | $7,000 |
| New conventional install, 4-bed home, mixed soil | $7,000 – $10,000 | $8,500 |

Numbers come from Out West Septic jobs in Palo Pinto, Hood, Erath, Stephens, Young, Jack, and Eastland counties over 2024–2026.

## What drives the cost

In roughly this order:

1. **Tank size** — bigger tank, more cost
2. **Drainfield length** — the big swing factor
3. **Soil** — rock, caliche, limestone all add cost
4. **Site access** — tight driveways, long hauls, steep slopes
5. **Permit fees** — county pass-through

## What adds cost on a real job

- **Hitting rock** — limestone shelves common in Palo Pinto County. Adder is itemized on the quote.
- **Slope work** — drainfield on a slope requires careful layout, sometimes terracing.
- **Tight access** — small equipment or hand-dig if excavator can't reach.
- **Long haul-off** — spoils have to go far.
- **Permit complexity** — BRA Possum Kingdom Lake jurisdiction adds steps.

## What an inspection costs

A real estate septic inspection in North Texas runs **$250–$500**. Out West Septic's inspection includes:

- Visual inspection of the tank and drainfield
- Water level, baffles, risers
- Distribution box check
- Setback compliance check
- Permit-on-file verification
- Written report with Pass / Conditional Pass / Fail verdict

## Hidden costs to budget for

Beyond the install or repair number:

- **Initial pump truck visit** — referred to a local pumper, $200–$400
- **First-year maintenance** — see homeowner maintenance guide
- **Slope restoration** — topsoil and seed usually included
- **Power** — conventional has none, aerobic has continuous draw

## The honest scope

These ranges are for **planning**, not for quotes. The actual number depends on the soil, the access, and whether you hit rock. Get a real quote from a TCEQ-licensed installer after a site visit. Out West Septic does free site visits for quotes.
