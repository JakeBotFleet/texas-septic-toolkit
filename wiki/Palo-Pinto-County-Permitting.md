# Palo Pinto County Permitting

Palo Pinto County OSSF permits are issued by the Palo Pinto County Public Works Department. Applications require a site plan, perc test, design calculations, and a county pass-through fee (currently $200-$400). DR review typically takes 1-3 weeks. Possum Kingdom Lake parcels within 2,000 ft of the lake or the 1,000-ft contour line fall under Brazos River Authority (BRA) jurisdiction instead.

## The permitting authority

Palo Pinto County is an "authorized county" under TCEQ. The Palo Pinto County Public Works Department is the local OSSF permitting authority.

- Office: Palo Pinto County Courthouse annex, 520 Oak Street, Palo Pinto, TX
- Phone: (940) 659-1272 (verify current number before relying)
- Hours: Mon to Fri 8:00am to 4:30pm

Always confirm the current DR name, hours, and fee schedule before submitting.

## What the application requires

1. Site plan drawn to scale (typically 1" = 20'), showing property lines, structures, proposed tank and drainfield locations, water wells, surface water, driveways, and easements.
2. Perc test report signed by a TCEQ-licensed Installer I, II, or III.
3. Design calculations. Daily design flow (150 gpd × bedrooms), tank size, absorption area, lateral count, lateral length.
4. Fee. Current county pass-through fee.

Out West Septic handles the application paperwork as part of every quote.

## The 10-step process

1. Site evaluation
2. Soil or perc test
3. Site plan
4. Application submitted to Public Works
5. Permit fee paid
6. DR review (1-3 weeks)
7. Install
8. Notify DR for inspection
9. DR inspection before backfill
10. Completion paperwork filed

## Common rejection reasons

- Incomplete setbacks (especially easements)
- Perc rate too slow for conventional
- Perc rate too fast
- Tank size wrong for bedroom count
- Insufficient lot size
- Surface water within 50 ft
- No TCEQ-licensed installer signature

## Possum Kingdom Lake parcels

Properties within 2,000 ft of Possum Kingdom Lake at normal pool, or within the 1,000-ft contour line, are under Brazos River Authority OSSF jurisdiction. Out West Septic does the conventional components and refers aerobic work inside this boundary.
