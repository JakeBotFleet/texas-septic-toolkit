# North Texas Soil Series Reference

> **Quick answer:** Out West Septic sees eight soil series regularly in rural North Texas — Cisco, Thurber, Bonti-Exray, Frio, Truce, Paluxy, Windthorst, and Duffau. Each has a typical perc rate and design implication. The perc test on the actual lot is the design input; the soil series is shorthand for what to expect.

## The eight series we see most

### Cisco — sandy loam, well drained

- **Texture:** sandy loam
- **Drainage:** well drained
- **Typical perc:** 8 min/in
- **Bedrock:** 60+ in
- **Septic suitability:** good — easy conventional
- **Counties:** Palo Pinto, Eastland

### Thurber — clay, moderately well drained

- **Texture:** clay
- **Drainage:** moderately well drained
- **Typical perc:** 45 min/in
- **Bedrock:** 36 in
- **Septic suitability:** marginal — slow perc; expanded drainfield or aerobic referral
- **Counties:** Palo Pinto, Stephens

### Bonti-Exray — rocky / limestone

- **Texture:** rocky / limestone
- **Drainage:** excessively drained
- **Typical perc:** 12 min/in
- **Bedrock:** 14 in
- **Septic suitability:** shallow — depth to limestone is the constraint
- **Counties:** Palo Pinto, Erath

### Frio — silty clay loam, somewhat poorly drained

- **Texture:** silty clay loam
- **Drainage:** somewhat poorly drained
- **Typical perc:** 25 min/in
- **Bedrock:** 48 in
- **Septic suitability:** bottomland — seasonal water table can be the constraint
- **Counties:** Palo Pinto, Hood

### Truce — clay loam, moderately well drained

- **Texture:** clay loam
- **Drainage:** moderately well drained
- **Typical perc:** 20 min/in
- **Bedrock:** 42 in
- **Septic suitability:** workable — standard conventional with care on setbacks
- **Counties:** Palo Pinto, Stephens

### Paluxy — sandy, well drained

- **Texture:** sandy
- **Drainage:** well drained
- **Typical perc:** 10 min/in
- **Bedrock:** 60+ in
- **Septic suitability:** good — sandy; watch water table near lakes
- **Counties:** Hood

### Windthorst — clay, moderately well drained

- **Texture:** clay
- **Drainage:** moderately well drained
- **Typical perc:** 40 min/in
- **Bedrock:** 30 in
- **Septic suitability:** marginal — slow perc; expanded drainfield
- **Counties:** Hood, Erath

### Duffau — sandy loam, well drained

- **Texture:** sandy loam
- **Drainage:** well drained
- **Typical perc:** 12 min/in
- **Bedrock:** 48 in
- **Septic suitability:** good — standard conventional design
- **Counties:** Hood, Erath

## How to look up the soil on your lot

USDA NRCS **Web Soil Survey** (https://websoilsurvey.sc.egov.usda.gov/App/HomePage.htm) is free and public. Look up the property by address or by drawing the parcel on the map. The map unit will list the soil series and typical perc rate range.
