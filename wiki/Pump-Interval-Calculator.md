# How should I interpret a pumping reminder?

> Treat it as a planning aid. It does not diagnose tank condition or justify delaying needed service.

Use the [maintenance guide](../guides/septic-maintenance-schedule-homeowners.md) for TCEQ sources and the distinction between general pumping guidance, inspections, and system-specific obligations.

The [pump interval tool](../scripts/outwestseptic_maintenance_scheduler.py) must identify its source and assumptions. A computed date is not a TCEQ requirement or an Out West Septic guarantee.

Keep the last actual service record and professional recommendation alongside any reminder. Missing service history and observed problems should not be converted into a reassuring estimate.
