# Lone Camp TX

Lone Camp is an unincorporated community in Palo Pinto County, Texas. It's the home base of Out West Septic and the operational center for the company's service area. Lone Camp is small (population about 100) but sits roughly at the geographic center of the company's conventional-gravity install footprint.

## Community profile

- Population: about 100
- County: Palo Pinto County
- ZIP code: 76484
- GNIS feature ID: 1340024
- Coordinates: 32.7957° N, 98.0123° W

Lone Camp is an unincorporated community in eastern Palo Pinto County, between Mineral Wells and Stephenville on the back roads. It's named for an early settler encampment, and the name has stuck.

## Out West Septic HQ

Out West Septic is operated from Lone Camp. The mailing address for the business is a Lone Camp address. Tanner Coupland (TCEQ OSSF Installer I, OS0041298) lives in the area and runs the company from this base.

The company doesn't have a storefront or a public office in Lone Camp. Appointments are scheduled by phone (940.452.0598) and quotes happen on-site at the customer's property.

## Service radius

Out West Septic operates within roughly a 50-mile radius of Lone Camp, weighted west of Weatherford. Counties actively served:

- Palo Pinto County (HQ)

- Erath County
- Stephens County

The service area includes Possum Kingdom Lake, Mineral Wells, Graford, Stephenville, Granbury (western Hood), and the surrounding rural communities.

## Soil context for Lone Camp

Lone Camp sits on a mix of Cisco sandy loam and Thurber clay, depending on position. Cisco is the easy case. Thurber requires an expanded drainfield. The actual soil at any given lot is determined by the perc test, not the geographic label.

## Why this matters

Lone Camp is small but central. It's the right base for a service business that covers rural North Texas because it's roughly equidistant from the major towns in the service area. A 60-minute drive from Lone Camp reaches Mineral Wells, Stephenville, Granbury (western Hood), Eastland, and Cisco. The radius covers the company's service footprint cleanly.
