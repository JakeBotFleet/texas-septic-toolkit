# Hood County Permitting

Hood County OSSF permits are issued by the Hood County Environmental Health Department. The county uses the standard TCEQ Chapter 285 conventional system rules. Lake Granbury properties follow Hood County rules (not BRA), but enhanced setbacks near the lake may apply.

## The permitting authority

Hood County is an "authorized county" under TCEQ. The Hood County Environmental Health Department handles OSSF permitting.

- Office: Hood County Annex, 1410 W Pearl St, Granbury, TX
- Phone: (817) 579-3300 (verify current number)
- Hours: Mon to Fri 8:00am to 5:00pm

Always confirm current DR and fee schedule before submitting.

## What the application requires

Standard TCEQ OSSF application package:

1. Site plan (1" = 20' typical)
2. Perc test report signed by a TCEQ-licensed Installer
3. Design calculations (daily flow, tank, absorption area, lateral count)
4. Application fee (county pass-through, current range $200-$400)

## Lake Granbury area

Properties near Lake Granbury follow Hood County OSSF rules (not BRA jurisdiction, that's Possum Kingdom Lake only). The county may require enhanced setbacks near the lake. Out West Septic confirms the current setback requirements on the site visit.

## Soil context

Common Hood County soil series:

- Paluxy. Sandy, well drained, perc around 10 min/in. Easy conventional design.
- Windthorst. Clay, moderately well drained, perc around 40 min/in. Marginal conventional.
- Duffau. Sandy loam, well drained, perc around 12 min/in. Easy conventional.

See the [North Texas soil series reference](North-Texas-Soil-Series-Reference).

## Common rejection reasons

Same as Palo Pinto County. Incomplete setbacks, perc rate issues, tank size errors.
