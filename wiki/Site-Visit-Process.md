# What should I bring to an OSSF site discussion?

> See the [Project preparation checklist](../guides/tceq-ossf-permitting-palo-pinto-county.md) for the maintained guidance and primary sources.

Bring the parcel/survey information, proposed use, existing permits and system records, and questions about evaluation and planning responsibilities. A sales visit is not necessarily a qualified site evaluation.
