# What does the tank-volume calculation establish?

> It checks a liquid-volume formula using a documented daily-flow input. It does not choose that flow or approve a tank.

See [tool inputs, formula provenance, and current-rule verification limits](../guides/tool-inputs-and-sources.md) for the dated source and calculation table.

Use the [tank sizing script](../scripts/outwestseptic_tank_sizer.py) with the input requirements shown by its help. Retain the flow source and output with the project record.

Identify the applicable flow category and supporting building/use information instead of assuming a universal gallons-per-bedroom multiplier. Septic tank liquid volume and aerobic treatment capacity are different quantities. Product suitability, compartment/access details, and other requirements need separate review.
