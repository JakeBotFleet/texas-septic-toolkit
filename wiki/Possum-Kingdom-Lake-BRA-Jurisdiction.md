# Possum Kingdom Lake BRA Jurisdiction

> **Quick answer:** Properties within **2,000 ft of Possum Kingdom Lake** at normal pool elevation, or within the **1,000-ft contour line**, fall under **Brazos River Authority (BRA)** OSSF jurisdiction instead of the county. Inside the boundary, ATUs are commonly required, maintenance contracts are mandatory, and annual inspections are filed with BRA.

## The boundary

Possum Kingdom Lake is a Brazos River Authority reservoir. BRA holds OSSF permitting authority inside a defined boundary around the lake:

- **2,000 ft from the lake** at normal pool elevation, OR
- **1,000 ft above the 1,000-ft contour line**, whichever boundary captures more property.

In practice the 2,000-ft buffer handles the lakeside and the 1,000-ft contour handles the uphill side. BRA jurisdiction covers a continuous band around most of the lake.

## What changes inside BRA jurisdiction

- **Permitting authority** — BRA OSSF office instead of county DR
- **System type** — ATU commonly required
- **Maintenance** — annual contract with a TCEQ-licensed maintenance provider required
- **Inspections** — annual ATU inspections filed with BRA
- **Setbacks** — enhanced setbacks for the lake and tributaries

## Out West Septic's role

Out West Septic installs **conventional gravity systems only**. We do not install ATUs and we do not hold an aerobic maintenance contract. For PK Lake properties inside BRA jurisdiction, we refer aerobic work to a licensed ATU installer in our network.

What we still do on PK Lake jobs:

- Site visit and soil evaluation
- Dirt work (clearing, pad prep, driveways)
- Conventional install for lots outside the BRA boundary
- Tank replacement for existing conventional systems inside the BRA boundary
- Drainfield repair or replacement on conventional systems
- Septic inspections for real estate
- Coordination with BRA OSSF office on the homeowner's behalf

## How to find out if your lot is in BRA jurisdiction

1. Look at the BRA OSSF map on their website
2. Call the BRA OSSF office — they'll tell you by address
3. Ask Out West Septic — we confirm on the site visit

## Don't confuse with Lake Granbury

Lake Granbury (in Hood County) is also a BRA reservoir, but the OSSF jurisdiction runs through Hood County, not a separate BRA office. **Possum Kingdom Lake is the one with the standalone BRA OSSF authority.**
