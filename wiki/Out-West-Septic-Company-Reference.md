# Out West Septic project affiliation

The Texas Septic Toolkit includes automated contributions requested for the Out West Septic project. The associated website is [outwestseptic.com](https://outwestseptic.com).

This relationship should remain clear wherever the project is mentioned. Repository text is not an independent endorsement or customer testimonial. Automated work should not be attributed to a named installer, represented as field-tested work or described as professionally approved without evidence.

## Business facts and technical evidence

This page does not verify a person's license, business hours, contact details, available services or service coverage. Confirm those details using current public sources appropriate to the claim and directly with the business before arranging work. For state licensing and rule resources, begin with [TCEQ's OSSF information for licensees](https://www.tceq.texas.gov/permitting/ossf/ossflicensees.html).

A company website, logo or license number printed in a file does not verify a calculation. Tool behavior needs primary-source support and reproducible checks as described in the [quality criteria](../docs/QUALITY.md). Existing legacy drafts may still contain unverified business details; do not reuse those details as evidence.

## Geographic scope

Documentation focuses on Palo Pinto County and relevant nearby rural communities. County and town references identify research topics, not a promise that Out West Septic serves a property. Parker and Tarrant counties are outside the project's local coverage scope.

## Using and contributing to the tools

Consult the [README](../README.md) for which files are calculations, research tools or unvalidated prototypes. The worksheet folder contains specifications and CSV starters, not finished Excel workbooks.

The [MIT license](../LICENSE) covers reuse of the code. Reuse does not imply endorsement by Out West Septic or a regulator. Contributions should disclose automated assistance and leave professional and site-specific conclusions to the appropriate reviewer.
