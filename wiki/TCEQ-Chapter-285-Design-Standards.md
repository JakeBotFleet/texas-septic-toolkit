# How should I verify a septic design claim?

> Trace each claim to the applicable current rule, source edition, site evidence, and local requirement.

Use the [tool input and source guide](../guides/tool-inputs-and-sources.md) for formula provenance and unresolved current-rule verification. Use the [setback source checklist](TCEQ-Chapter-285-Setbacks-Table.md) for dimensions, and [soil research guide](../guides/palo-pinto-county-soil-types-septic.md) for evidence limits.

TCEQ's [system-selection guidance](https://www.tceq.texas.gov/permitting/ossf/ossfsystems.html) distinguishes treatment and disposal methods and their requirements. A tool name, output label, or repository citation cannot certify a complete design.
