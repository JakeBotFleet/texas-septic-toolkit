# Erath County Permitting

Erath County OSSF permits are issued by the Erath County Environmental Health Department. The county uses standard TCEQ Chapter 285 conventional system rules. Stephenville and Dublin are the major population centers in the service area.

## The permitting authority

Erath County is an "authorized county" under TCEQ. The Erath County Environmental Health Department handles OSSF permitting.

- Office: Erath County Courthouse, 100 W. Grafton St., Stephenville, TX
- Phone: (254) 965-1489 (verify current number)
- Hours: Mon to Fri 8:00am to 5:00pm

Always confirm current DR and fee schedule before submitting.

## What the application requires

Standard TCEQ OSSF application package:

1. Site plan
2. Perc test report signed by a TCEQ-licensed Installer
3. Design calculations
4. Application fee (county pass-through, typically $200-$400)

## Soil context

Common Erath County soil series:

- Bonti-Exray. Rocky / limestone, perc around 12 min/in. Shallow limestone is the design constraint.
- Windthorst. Clay, perc around 40 min/in. Marginal conventional.
- Duffau. Sandy loam, perc around 12 min/in. Easy conventional.

## Towns served

- Stephenville TX. County seat.
- Dublin TX.
- Hico TX (southern Erath).
- Lingleville TX.
- Morgan Mill TX.
