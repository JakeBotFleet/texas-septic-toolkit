# Real Estate Inspection Process

A real estate septic inspection in rural North Texas takes 60-90 minutes on site and costs $250-$500. Out West Septic signs the report as TCEQ OSSF Installer I (OS0041298). The verdict is Pass / Conditional Pass / Fail, with line items and recommended next steps. 48-hour report turnaround.

## Who orders the inspection

In a typical rural Texas real estate transaction:

- The buyer orders the inspection, often through their realtor
- The seller usually pays if there's an inspection contingency
- The lender may require it for properties on more than a few acres
- The title company usually doesn't require it but it should be on the buyer's checklist

Make sure the person signing the inspection report is a current TCEQ OSSF Installer I, II, or III. Out West Septic (OS0041298) signs every inspection report we issue.

## What we inspect

### The tank

- Tank location, size, material
- Visible cracks, baffles, inlet/outlet
- Water level
- Sludge and scum depth
- Risers present (recommend install if not)

### The drainfield

- Drainfield type and approximate age
- Surfacing wet spots
- Odors
- Unusually green grass over the field
- Backup into the house or to the surface
- Distribution box condition
- Inspection ports

### The site

- Surface water within 50 ft
- Setbacks to well, property line, foundation, surface water, utility lines
- Recent heavy rain or heavy use

### Code compliance

- Permit on file with the county DR
- Tank and drainfield setback compliance with TCEQ Chapter 285

## The report

A one-to-two-page PDF on Out West Septic letterhead with:

1. Header. Out West Septic branding, TCEQ OSSF Installer I, license OS0041298.
2. Property info. Address, county, inspection date, weather.
3. Tank section.
4. Drainfield section.
5. Site section.
6. Verdict. Pass / Conditional Pass / Fail.
7. Recommended next steps.
8. Signature: Tanner Coupland (OS0041298).

## The verdicts

- Pass. System is functioning as designed. Routine maintenance.
- Conditional Pass. Functioning but has issues to address soon.
- Fail. System is not functioning as designed. Specific recommended repairs with cost estimate.

## What we don't cover

The real estate inspection is not a perc test, a tank pump, or a full system replacement quote. Those are separate scopes:

- Perc test. For new construction or system redesign.
- Tank pump. Referred to a local pumper.
- Full system replacement quote. Separate site visit and perc test.
