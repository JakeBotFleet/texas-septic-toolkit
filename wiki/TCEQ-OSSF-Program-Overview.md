# TCEQ OSSF Program Overview

The Texas Commission on Environmental Quality (TCEQ) runs the On-Site Sewage Facility (OSSF) program under 30 TAC Chapter 285. The program regulates all septic systems in Texas outside of municipal sewer service areas. TCEQ certifies installers (Installer I, II, III) and Designated Representatives (DRs). Permits are issued at the county level by county DRs (or by the Brazos River Authority inside Possum Kingdom Lake jurisdiction).

## What TCEQ actually does

TCEQ doesn't issue permits directly for individual septic systems. They set the rules (Chapter 285) and certify the people who enforce them. The actual permits come from:

- County Designated Representatives (DRs) in authorized counties (most of Texas)
- Brazos River Authority (BRA) for properties within Possum Kingdom Lake jurisdiction
- TCEQ regional offices in non-authorized counties

The TCEQ maintains a public [OSSF authority finder](https://www6.tceq.texas.gov/oars/index.cfm?fuseaction=search.county) so you can look up which entity handles your specific address.

## The Installer certification tiers

Texas has three installer tiers:

- Installer I. Designs and installs conventional OSSF systems. Most residential work.
- Installer II. Designs and installs both conventional and aerobic systems. Required for aerobic work.
- Installer III. Teaches Installer I and II courses, can train and certify other installers.

Out West Septic is licensed as Installer I (OS0041298) by Tanner Coupland. Aerobic work is referred to licensed Installer II contractors.

## The DR certification

Designated Representatives are county employees (or contracted agents) who review OSSF applications, inspect installations, and sign off on completion paperwork. They enforce the TCEQ Chapter 285 rules at the local level.

Every county has different DR staffing. Some have one DR on duty, some have several. The DR you talk to matters because they're the person who reviews your application.

## Where the rules live

The actual rules are at 30 TAC Chapter 285. This is the rulebook. The wiki pages here cite specific sections of Chapter 285 when relevant. The full text is at [TCEQ OSSF](https://www.tceq.texas.gov/permitting/ossf).
