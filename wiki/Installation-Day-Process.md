# Installation Day Process

> **Quick answer:** A conventional septic install takes 1–3 days. Day 1: tank hole, tank set, lateral trenching. Day 2: lateral pipe and gravel, distribution box, inspection ports, DR inspection. Day 3: backfill, rough grade, topsoil, seed. Heavy equipment on site 2–4 days.

## Before install day

By the time we arrive:

- Permit approved by the county DR (or BRA, if PK Lake)
- Site plan finalized and staked
- Access path clear from driveway to tank and drainfield areas
- Building pad prepped (if part of the same scope)
- Customer or representative available by phone

## Day 1 — Tank hole and lateral trenches

We arrive with:

- 20-ton excavator
- Dump truck or trailer
- Hand tools, transit, tape measure
- Pipe, gravel, fabric, distribution box

The morning sequence:

1. Walk the site to confirm tank and drainfield locations
2. Excavate the tank hole
3. Set the tank in the hole using the excavator. Level it.
4. Excavate the lateral trenches
5. Lay the pipe in the trenches
6. Set the distribution box
7. Place the inspection ports

## Day 2 — DR inspection and prep

1. Lay the gravel in the trenches
2. Install the geotextile fabric
3. Connect distribution box to the laterals
4. Call DR for inspection
5. DR walks the install (1–3 business day lead time)

## Day 3 — Backfill and restoration

1. Backfill the tank hole
2. Backfill the lateral trenches
3. Rough grade the disturbed area
4. Topsoil over the disturbed area
5. Seed and straw

## If we hit rock

Out West Septic stops and **calls you** before continuing. The rock adder is itemized on every quote. Outcomes:

- **Shallow rock in the tank hole** — deeper excavation or relocation
- **Rock in the lateral trenches** — rock adder per cubic yard
- **Significant rock across the site** — redesign conversation

## After install

- Water use is normal
- **Don't drive over tank or drainfield**
- **Don't plant trees over the drainfield**
- **Watch the seed** — water if needed
- **Call with questions** — Tanner at 940.452.0598
