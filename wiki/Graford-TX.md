# Graford TX

Graford is in Palo Pinto County, Texas, just east of Possum Kingdom Lake. OSSF permits route through the Palo Pinto County Public Works Department, except for properties inside the BRA OSSF jurisdiction near PK Lake. Out West Septic serves Graford for conventional gravity septic installation, repair, and inspections.

## City profile

- Population: about 600
- County: Palo Pinto County
- ZIP code: 76449
- GNIS feature ID: 1358177
- Coordinates: 32.9379° N, 98.2467° W
- Distance from Out West Septic HQ (Lone Camp): about 15 miles

Graford is the closest town to the east side of Possum Kingdom Lake. It's an unincorporated community that serves as a gateway to PK Lake from the south and east. The town has a post office, a school district, and a handful of small businesses.

## Possum Kingdom Lake proximity

Graford is inside the PK Lake area. Many properties in the Graford area are within 2,000 ft of PK Lake or the 1,000-ft contour line, which puts them in BRA OSSF jurisdiction rather than the Palo Pinto County system. Out West Septic confirms jurisdiction at the site visit.

## Common OSSF scenarios in Graford

- PK Lake properties. Most Graford-area OSSF work is for lake properties. ATUs commonly required inside the BRA boundary.
- Rural ranch properties. Outside the BRA boundary, conventional systems are standard.
- New construction. Small volume of new builds in the Graford area.
