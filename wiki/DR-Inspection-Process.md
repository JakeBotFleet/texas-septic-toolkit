# DR Inspection Process

> **Quick answer:** The Designated Representative (DR) inspects the septic system before backfill is complete. The DR checks tank location and setbacks, tank size and material, lateral layout, distribution box, and setback compliance. The DR signs off and files the completion paperwork with TCEQ.

## When the DR inspects

After Out West Septic has installed the system but **before backfill is complete**. The DR must see the open tank hole, the open lateral trenches, and the distribution box.

The DR inspection happens at Out West Septic's request. We call the DR's office when the install is ready. Lead time is typically **1–3 business days** depending on DR workload.

## What the DR checks

- **Tank location** — matches the approved site plan
- **Tank size and material** — matches the approved application
- **Tank setbacks** — confirmed in the field
- **Lateral layout** — length, count, depth, spacing match the approved design
- **Distribution box** — level, accessible
- **Inspection ports** — present and accessible
- **Setbacks** — confirmed in the field for the actual install

## What happens if the DR finds an issue

The DR can:

- **Pass** — sign off, file completion paperwork
- **Conditional pass** — sign off with conditions (e.g., move a lateral, add an inspection port). Out West Septic addresses the conditions before backfill.
- **Fail** — require correction before sign-off. Rare in our experience.

Conditional pass is the most common outcome. The conditions are usually minor (move a port, add a marker) and addressed within hours.

## After DR sign-off

The DR files a completion report with TCEQ. The homeowner keeps a copy for resale, refinancing, or insurance. Out West Septic keeps a copy on file.

## What the homeowner sees

The DR usually does not require the homeowner to be present. Out West Septic schedules the inspection, meets the DR on site, walks the install, and gets the sign-off. The homeowner gets the completion paperwork in the mail or via email.
