# How should I prepare for the authority's OSSF inspection?

> Ask the permitting authority what it must inspect, when to request inspection, and what may be covered afterward.

Confirm the plan revision, inspection contact/process, required visible components, correction handling, and completion documents before installation. Keep the authority's directions and records with the project file.

Ask the office for its actual scheduling and correction process. This repository does not establish local lead times or inspection outcomes.

Start with [TCEQ permitting guidance](https://www.tceq.texas.gov/permitting/ossf/ossfpermits.html), then use the parcel's authority and approved plan. For Palo Pinto, see the [county preparation guide](../guides/tceq-ossf-permitting-palo-pinto-county.md).
